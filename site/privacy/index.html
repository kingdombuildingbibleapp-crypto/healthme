Privacy Policy

HealthyScan is built so your health information stays under your control. By default it stays on your device. If you choose to create an account, your profiles are encrypted on your device before they're saved, and we can't read them.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter or import is stored in your phone's or computer's browser storage on that device. That includes profiles, family members, profile photos, conditions, allergies, medications, findings from medical records, and scan history. Without an account, none of it is sent to us.

Optional account (encrypted)

You can create an account so your profiles come back on another phone or after reinstalling. Here's how it works:

  • Encrypted before it leaves your device. Your profiles are encrypted on your device with a random key (AES-256-GCM). That key is itself locked twice on your device: once with your password and once with a recovery code. We store only these encrypted pieces and one-way hashes used to check your sign-in. We never receive your password or recovery code, and we can't decrypt your profiles.
  • We don't store your email address. Your account is identified by a one-way hash of your email.
  • Forgot your password? Use the recovery code you saved when you created your account to set a new password. If you're still signed in on any device, you can also change your password there. Without your password, a recovery code or a signed-in device, we can't unlock your account copy, because we don't hold the keys. The copy on your phone is not affected.
  • Where it's stored. Encrypted data is stored with our hosting provider, Netlify, in the United States.
  • Delete anytime. "Delete my account" in the app permanently erases your account and its encrypted data from our servers.

Medical records and patient portals

  • Uploaded files and photos (PDF, images, CCD/XML, FHIR JSON, text) are read by software running in your browser. The file and its text are discarded after analysis. Only the items you approve are saved, on your device.
  • Patient portal connection (SMART on FHIR). If you connect a patient portal such as MyChart, you sign in on your health system's own website and approve read-only access. Your browser then downloads your problem list, allergies, medications and recent results directly from your health system to your device. HealthyScan's servers are not involved. The access token is used once and then deleted. We never write to your medical record.

What leaves your device

  • Barcode lookups. When you scan a product, its barcode number is sent to open product databases (Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Pet Food Facts, Open Products Facts). If none of those has it, the number goes to UPCitemdb through our server. Only the barcode number is sent, never your health information.
  • Website hosting. Our host (Netlify) processes standard technical data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver the website.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your health information. Accounts are optional, and account data is encrypted so that only your password can unlock it.

Your controls

  • Remove conditions or delete profiles at any time in the app.
  • "Remove everything added from records" clears imported findings.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for healthyscan.net deletes everything on that device.
  • "Delete my account" erases your encrypted account copy from our servers.
  • Backup files you save contain health information. Store them securely.

HIPAA and health systems

When you choose HealthyScan yourself, we are not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate. If a health system offers HealthyScan to its patients under a contract, we will enter into a Business Associate Agreement and protect any protected health information we handle as HIPAA requires. In the event of a breach of health information we hold, we will notify affected people as required by law, including the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.

Children

Parents and guardians may create profiles for children on their own device. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and child profiles never leave the device.
