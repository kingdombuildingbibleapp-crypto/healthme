Epic on FHIR registration worksheet

Copy these values into fhir.epic.com → Build Apps → Create. Field names may differ slightly from Epic's current form.

Before you start

  • Sign up at fhir.epic.com with partners@healthyscan.net or your own email. You'll need to verify the email address.
  • These public pages must load, because Epic asks for them: healthyscan.net/privacy/ and healthyscan.net/terms/.

After saving

  1. Copy the Non-Production Client ID and the Production Client ID.
  2. Send both to your developer. They're public identifiers, not secrets, and safe to email. They go in /js/ehr.js to switch on "Epic test patients" and production MyChart connections.
  3. Test with Epic's sandbox test patients (credentials are listed on fhir.epic.com under "Test Data").
  4. Mark the app Ready for Production when sandbox testing passes.

If the form won't let you continue

  • "Redirect URI invalid": it must be HTTPS and match exactly, including the trailing slash.
  • Can't select automatic distribution: remove any API that isn't a USCDI read API.
  • It asks for a public key or JWKS: you've picked a backend or confidential app type. Choose Patients as the audience and turn off confidential client.
  • Account pending or verification: check the inbox and spam folder of the email you signed up with.
